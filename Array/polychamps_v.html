The tournament

On Saturday the 16th of November, all the players of ETH Zürich and EPF Lausanne will once again face off in a highly competitive chess match, the

POLYCHAMPS V

The match has a unique hybrid format where the top 8 players of each university will play a classically time controlled team match in Zürich. A blitz lichess arena opposing all chess enthusiasts of both universities will be played online at the same time. It is free to join and open to all levels for members of ETHZ and EPFL. Details on registration will be communicated later. The specific rules are described on the rules page. All the action will be live streamed and commented on Twitch.

How to register?

Follow these simple steps to take part in the online tournament.
It's free and for all levels so don't hesitate to register.

1. Create a free account or login on Lichess

2. Join your university Team

Please indicate your name and university email when joining any team:

  • La Dame Blanche (EPFL)
  • SKETHZ (ETHZ)

It might take some time to get accepted in the team. Once it's done, you should receive a notification. We will accept the players incrementally so that the teams are roughly similar, register as soon as possible to secure your spot!

3. Register to the tournament

Simply join the tournament on the tournament page. Once you've done this, you're all set!
If you have any questions, ask your questions in the tournament chat, refer to the rules or contact us directly here.

4. Play

On gameday (16th of November), be sure to connect on the tournament page a bit before 14:30. Then relax and take this opportunity to watch the Livestream. Once it's go time, let yourself be guided by the website. Good luck!
